## Brand & Style
The brand bridges the wonder of storybook fantasy with high-end botanical commerce. The emotional tone evokes wonder, organic elegance, warmth, and nostalgia—akin to stepping through a wrought-iron gate into a moonlit, bioluminescent conservatory.

The design aesthetic blends **Organic Tactility** with **Luminous Glassmorphism** and refined storybook ornamentation:
- Warm parchment and alabaster surfaces form the foundation.
- Deep verdant tones evoke ancient forests and greenhouse glass.
- Radiant starlight golds, soft amber motes, and twilight amethyst provide enchanted accents.
- Delicate gilded filigree, soft atmospheric illumination, and glass-like translucent containers frame content without visual clutter.
- The interface serves both customer-facing wonder (immersive browsing, storytelling product showcases) and an organized, regal back-office administration suite (inventory, greenhouse analytics, botanical lifecycle tracking).

## Colors
The color palette reflects deep enchanted foliage kissed by warm golden starlight and twilight blooms:

- **Primary (`#1B4332` - Deep Sylvan Green):** Grounding emerald base representing lush royal conservatories, towering topiary, and ancient flora. Used for prominent headers, primary action buttons, deep UI containers, and navigational foundations.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37` - Starlight Filigree Gold):** Luminous fairy-tale metallic used for focus rings, magic accents, active states, key interactive indicators, and delicate borders.
- **Tertiary (`#4A2840` - Enchanted Plum / Royal Nightshade):** Deep velvet violet supporting rare specimens, status indicators, badges, and regal backoffice telemetry alerts.
- **Neutral (`#212529` - Bramble Charcoal):** Deep woodland tone used for primary typography to ensure high contrast and readability against parchment surfaces.
- **Canvas & Surface Tiers:**
  - Base Canvas: `#FAF7F0` (Pressed Warm Linen).
  - Surface Raised: `#FFFFFF` (Alabaster Glass with 92% opacity).
  - Surface Subdued: `#F0EBE1` (Aged Vellum).
  - Starlight Glow Accent: `#FFF6D6` with soft outer luminance for hovering specimens and active toggles.

## Typography
The typographic architecture pairs the timeless, narrative cadence of **Playfair Display** with the clear, ergonomic legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**:

- **Display & Headlines (Playfair Display):** Imparts fairy-tale majesty, bookish sophistication, and historic charm. Used for prominent landing page banners, plant titles, botanical family names, and high-level dashboard summaries.
- **Body & Numerical Data (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Balances romantic serifs with accessible, open geometry. Ensures dense tables, greenhouse climate meters, and care instructions remain readable.
- **Uppercase Labels:** Small labels, status chips, and overlines leverage generous letter-spacing (`0.04em` - `0.06em`) in small-caps or bold weights to mirror classic garden ledger typography.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a structured 12-column fluid grid system on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single- or double-column rhythm on mobile devices:

- **Desktop (1024px and up):** 12-column grid with a max container width of `1440px`. Column gutters are set to `1.5rem` (`24px`), with outer canvas margins of `3rem` (`48px`). In backoffice views, the left-hand royal navigation rail occupies a static `280px` width while the main workspace adapts dynamically.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` margins. Complex multi-metric cards wrap into 2x2 grids.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` screen margins. Hero text and botanical galleries switch to stacked single columns with horizontal swipe carousels for rare plant specimen showcases.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Rooted in an 8px baseline rhythm. Components utilize `space-md` (`16px`) for standard internal padding and `space-lg` (`24px`) to `space-xl` (`40px`) to delineate sections.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation evokes glowing enchanted foliage and layered glass conservatory panes:

- **Surface Translucency & Frosted Glass:** Card containers, floating sheets, and modals use semi-translucent backdrops (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`) with a blur filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) to mimic dew-kissed glass.
- **Fairy-Tale Amber & Emerald Shadows:** Rather than cold neutral grays, shadows incorporate subtle green and warm amber undertones:
  - *Resting Elevation (Level 1):* `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08)`.
  - *Hover Elevation (Level 2):* `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.12), 0 0 12px 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)`.
  - *Floating Modals & Flyouts (Level 3):* `0 16px 40px -6px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.22), 0 0 20px 2px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.
- **Gilded Borders:** Containers feature a fine `1px` border tinted with antique gold (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`) or soft forest leaf (`rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.12)`), catching light like antique brass plant vitrines.

## Shapes
The shape language uses organic, arched geometries reminiscent of palace greenhouse archways, smooth river stones, and curved leaves:

- **Corner Radius Level 2 (Rounded):** Standard elements (inputs, standard buttons, small data tiles) feature an `8px` (`0.5rem`) radius.
- **Cards & Content Windows (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`):** Feature generous `16px` (`1rem`) to `24px` (`1.5rem`) curves. Top corners on featured product containers may employ an arch motif (`28px` top, `12px` bottom) for cathedral conservatory styling.
- **Pills & Circular Badges:** Tags, status pills, and magical icon capsules maintain fully rounded borders (`9999px`) to contrast with structured text frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Enchanted Action"):** Solid background in `#1B4332`, crisp ivory text (`#FAF7F0`), `1px` border of `#D4AF37`. On hover, shifts to a slightly brighter sylvan tone with a diffuse warm gold starlight halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary ("Royal Filigree"):** Transparent background, border in `1.5px solid #D4AF37`, text in `#1B4332`. On hover, receives a soft wash of parchment amber (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`).
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Text in `#1B4332` with an underlined gold accent on hover and subtle sparkle icon transition.

### Cards & Specimen Tiles
- Framed in frosted glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`), accented with `1px` gold filigree trim (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`).
- Card headers feature botanical names set in Playfair Display accompanied by a botanical classification badge.
- Interactive cards lift slightly (`-4px`) on hover, casting an amber-tinted shadow.

### Input Fields & Select Controls
- Built on a lightly tinted linen base (`#FAF7F0`) with a `1px` border (`rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.2)`).
- Placeholder text in soft botanical moss (`#6B7A6E`).
- On focus, border transitions to `#D4AF37` accompanied by a soft golden glow ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`).

### Chips & Botanical Status Badges
- Elliptical pill shapes (`rounded-full`) with `12px` typography.
- **Magic Bloom Status:** Emerald tint (`#E8F3ED`) with `#1B4332` text.
- **Rare / Celestial:** Plum wash (`#F4EBF0`) with `#4A2840` text.
- **Sunlight / Vitality Warning:** Pale golden amber (`#FFF8E1`) with `#8A6708` text.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Checkboxes maintain an `8px` rounded square profile; radios are circular.
- Unchecked: `1.5px` border in `#6B7A6E` on an ivory surface.
- Checked: `#1B4332` fill displaying a custom warm gold (`#D4AF37`) checkmark or inner celestial dot.

### Specialized App Components
- **Magical Care Dial:** Circular humidity and starlight tracker utilizing dual concentric progress arcs (gold for light exposure, deep sylvan emerald for hydration).
- **Storybook Drawer:** Slide-out administrative panel styled with an aged parchment texture header, gold filigree close button, and tabbed foliage logs.
